How an acquired scan flows from physical hospital hardware to physician attestation in seconds.

01

Hardware Ingestion

Physical modalities push DICOM studies via DIMSE C-STORE to port 11112. File integrity is verified with Pydicom validation.

02

Neural Triage

Deep neural network executes multi-label inference in <1.2s. Critical tension pneumothorax or stroke triggers ACR Cat-1 priority queueing.

03

Physician Review

Radiologists manipulate Window/Level, apply false-color colormaps, measure pathology with sub-millimeter calipers, and scroll 3D MPR CT planes.

04

Attestation & Seal
